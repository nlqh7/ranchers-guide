Quick answer

Where do I find the map?

Use your character's smartphone and open the Map app. Owned vehicles can appear as map icons; outdoor farm animals can also appear while they are outside a barn or coop. For a lost or impounded vehicle, check My Garage and the Car Pound route.

Leafy Market

Officially described as a grocery chain carrying fresh produce, seeds and farming supplies. The permitted current-build map resources expose seven separate Leafy Market POIs around the city.

Interior confirmed in the current build Video-observed (Games Station, 03:15–03:55): the shop UI is split into four tabs — Seed & Fertilizer / Vegetables / Grocery / Weaponry (Temporary) — and the in-store scene shows the green leaf logo with a cashier NPC at the counter. Observed retail shelf prices remain purchase prices, not player sell prices; see the money-making guide for the current evidence boundary.

How to recognize it: enable the Shopping layer and look for the seven native grocery-store symbols. Selecting any branch opens this shared chain entry.

Auction market

The official site describes the auction market as a player-to-player trading market that is directly accessible from within the game and from the companion phone app — you open it from the in-game menu or the app rather than walking to a dedicated building.

We found no verified physical auction-house building location in the current Early Access build, so the map deliberately shows no pin here; the earlier fan-wiki-suggested position has been removed to avoid misleading directions. If you capture a current-build screenshot of a physical auction building, submit it with the map label and we will add a verified pin.

Subway entrances

The permitted Steam build 24847725 contains 16 active Subway POI trackers. Each tracker was independently matched within 5 metres to an active entrance model in its streamed world scene: 13 city entrances and three countryside entrances.

Count boundary: the official June update for version 0.8.10.420 says 15 travel stations. No later official note explains the additional current-build entrance, so these pins prove entrance placement—not that all 16 are independently selectable travel destinations.

Overnight Parking

The permitted Steam build 24847725 exposes 13 active SAFE_PARKING map trackers. They are the base object plus suffixes (2)–(13); there is no (1), so the count is based on enumerated objects rather than the highest suffix.

Completeness boundary: the official version 0.8.10.420 announcement says 17 Overnight Parking locations were added. The current build does not expose four additional SAFE_PARKING trackers, so these pins are exact for the 13 current trackers but are not presented as a complete 17-location set.
