Quick answer

Where do I find the map?

Use your character's smartphone and open the Map app. Owned vehicles can appear as map icons; outdoor farm animals can also appear while they are outside a barn or coop. For a lost or impounded vehicle, check My Garage and the Car Pound route.

Leafy Market

Officially described as a grocery chain carrying fresh produce, seeds and farming supplies, with multiple branches around the city center.

How to recognize it: look for the green leaf storefront logo. Because the official material shows a chain rather than one unique shop, choose the nearest visible branch and submit a phone-map screenshot if its current pin is readable.

Sources and limits

This directory was reviewed August 6, 2026. The road layout is retained from a current-build player walkthrough; official Steam updates support the named shops, districts and systems. Map pins on this page are approximate areas cross-checked against the fan wiki, not exact coordinates. A separate current player screenshot shows that the map panel can sometimes load blank, so this guide does not treat one capture as proof that the interface works for everyone. Exact shop pins, opening hours and stock remain unclaimed until a readable current-build label or route is retained.
