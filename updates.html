0.8.10.842: quest validation and ranch stability

Released August 21, this update targets quest progression, animal-to-building links and batch limits.

Answer first

If a quest will not advance, an animal loses its coop or barn association, shows the wrong hunger state after growing, or the linked count looks wrong, update to 0.8.10.842 or later before repeating an older workaround. The same announcement also raises shop purchase and crafting batch limits; it does not confirm every unrelated animal, building or economy report as fixed.

Current status: 0.8.10.842 is the latest official build recorded in the Steam news feed reviewed on August 22, 2026. A symptom that still reproduces after this update should be recorded with the new build number.
01

Quest progression

Several cases where quests failed to advance or validate correctly are included in the official fix list.

Barns and coops

The update covers lost associations, growth-stage transitions, incorrect hunger displays, linked counts, repeated turning and a same-frame disappearance case.

Shop and crafting batches

Stackable shop purchases now go up to 200, non-stackable purchases to 50, and crafting batches to 50.

Roadmap context

What the roadmap can and cannot tell you

The developer-shared Early Access roadmap is useful historical planning context, not a list of features currently available in 0.8.10.842.

Answer first

Use the current Steam announcements to decide what you can do in-game today. Treat roadmap dates, planned systems and older feature groupings as planned or historical until a later dated announcement or current-build test confirms them.

Safe reading: a roadmap item is not proof of a release date, a current unlock, or a working mechanic. Read the source note and its risk boundary, then return to the available-versus-planned guide.

Released August 10, this earlier hotfix remains useful when comparing the animal, vehicle and incubator paths it named.

Do this after updating

A short post-update check

This is a troubleshooting order, not a claim that every player must complete every step.

  1. Confirm the title-screen build. Steam normally updates automatically; check the number when a previously reported symptom remains.
  2. Load a safe save. Before moving animals or vehicles, keep the original save and note the count, location and state you can see.
  3. Repeat the smallest trigger. Test the exact transition that caused the problem: night entry, loading, subway travel, doorway movement or AutoHue entry.
  4. Separate fixed paths from new symptoms. A symptom that differs from the official list should not be labeled as the same bug without evidence.
  5. Send a complete report. Include build, platform, save context, steps, before/after state and a screenshot or clip through the contribution form.
Turn notes into action

How to use an update entry

Each entry answers three different questions so a patch note does not become a blanket promise about the whole game.

01

What changed?

Start with the official build and the exact systems named in the announcement. Older reports remain historical context when the patch covers their trigger.

02

What should I check?

Follow the smallest current-build test linked from the entry, keeping the original save and recording the before-and-after state.

03

What is still unknown?

Unmentioned systems keep their own evidence status. Use the research queue or community signals for open questions instead of inferring a fix.

What not to overread

Patch notes narrow the question; they do not answer every question

Version boundary: the 0.8.10.842 announcement targets quest validation, ranch links and batch limits. It does not verify exact crop prices, fertilizer calculations, greenhouse unlocking, map coordinates or every building-placement rule. Those topics keep their own evidence status.

That distinction matters for a live Early Access game. A fix for one disappearance path is not confirmation that all missing-animal reports share one cause. Likewise, an official roadmap item is not a current feature. Use the Early Access status guide for the broader available-versus-planned split, and the research queue for claims that still need a current-build test.
