Contact Us

Last updated: July 30, 2026

The fastest way to reach the team behind theranchersguide.com is email: hello@theranchersguide.com. We read everything, and corrections get priority.

Corrections & confirmed data

Found a mistake or a value we have not published? Email us with the subject line "Correction". Include the game version, what you did, where the value appeared, and a screenshot or short clip when possible.

Reader reports enter a verification queue first. We may ask for context or repeat the observation before updating a database. Contributors can be credited by name or remain anonymous.

Content feedback & requests

Want a guide we do not have yet? Tell us what you searched for, what decision you were trying to make, and what information was missing. Requests from real players set the writing and testing roadmap after release.

Business inquiries

For advertising, partnership, or press inquiries, email hello@theranchersguide.com with the subject line "Business". Note that this is an independent fan site; for anything concerning The Ranchers game itself, please contact RedPilz Studio or Trophy Games directly.

A quick note

We're a small fan project, not a company — replies can take a few days around the launch rush. Thanks for your patience, and happy ranching.
