Contact Us

Last updated: July 30, 2026

For general questions and site feedback, email hello@theranchersguide.com. Use the dedicated addresses below so research, corrections and business messages reach the right review queue.

Corrections & confirmed data

Found a mistake or a value we have not published? Email corrections@theranchersguide.com. Include the game version, what you did, where the value appeared, and a screenshot or short clip when possible.

Reader reports enter a verification queue first. We may ask for context or repeat the observation before updating a database. Contributors can be credited by name or remain anonymous.

Content feedback & requests

Want a guide we do not have yet? Email hello@theranchersguide.com and tell us what you searched for, what decision you were trying to make, and what information was missing. For a reproducible discovery, use the structured contribution form instead.

Business inquiries

For advertising, partnership, or press inquiries, email business@theranchersguide.com. Note that this is an independent fan site; for anything concerning The Ranchers game itself, please contact RedPilz Studio or Trophy Games directly.

A quick note

We're a small fan project, not a company — replies can take a few days around the launch rush. Thanks for your patience, and happy ranching.
