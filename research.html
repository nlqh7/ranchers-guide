Evidence ladder

What each status means

Status describes evidence strength, not how confident a sentence sounds. The full scale, verification workflow and conflict rule live on the methodology page.

Official

Published by the developer or visible as an explicit current-build rule.

Video-observed

Directly visible in video footage, cited with source and timestamp.

Community-confirmed

Two or more independent player reports agree under comparable conditions.

Player-tested

Measured hands-on in a named build, with retained evidence.

Unverified lead

A single-source clue. Useful as a test lead, not a settled fact.

Historical

Older Alpha, Beta or demo information that may no longer apply.

Obsolete

A later build changed or invalidated the earlier result.

How a report graduates

One observation starts the work

  1. Submit the build, platform, finding, method and source.
  2. We check whether the report is complete and legally usable.
  3. A matching independent observation promotes it to Community-confirmed.
  4. A retained reproduction can promote it to Player-tested.
  5. A later patch can move the record to Historical or Obsolete.
