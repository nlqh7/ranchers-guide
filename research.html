Evidence ladder

What each status means

Status describes evidence strength, not how confident a sentence sounds.

Official

Published by the developer or visible as an explicit current-build rule.

Verified

Reproduced by this site on the named build with retained evidence.

Corroborated

Two independent current-build reports agree under comparable conditions.

Reported

One traceable player report; useful as a test lead, not a settled fact.

Historical

Older Alpha, Beta or demo information that may no longer apply.

Obsolete

A later build changed or invalidated the earlier result.

How a report graduates

One observation starts the work

  1. Submit the build, platform, finding, method and source.
  2. We check whether the report is complete and legally usable.
  3. A matching independent observation promotes it to Corroborated.
  4. A retained reproduction can promote it to Verified.
  5. A later patch can move the record to Obsolete or Needs recheck.
