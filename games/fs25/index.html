Farming Simulator 25

This corner currently covers exactly one thing: the bread production chain (wheat → flour → bread). It does not reuse the Ranchers map, and it does not claim to cover every production chain, profit rankings or a full encyclopedia.

Bread Production Planner

Enter a monthly bread target and work backwards: flour and wheat per month, the field area to grow it, and whether one bakery and one grain mill can keep up — capacity gaps are flagged before you buy buildings.

Open the planner
Data boundary: chain values in the planner are community references and illustrative defaults, not yet runtime-confirmed on the current build, and they are labelled as such on the tool. Playing The Ranchers instead? Back to The Ranchers Guide.
