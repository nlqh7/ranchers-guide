Advanced: capacity, ratios & area assumptions sample data

These fields start blank so that no reference value is mistaken for confirmed data. Press “Load sample data” to fill every field from the sample values shipped with the data file — they stay illustrative sample values: not runtime-confirmed, and not independently sourced value by value. An invalid value (0, negative, non-numeric or out of range) blocks only the results that depend on it; independent outputs remain visible instead of silently substituting defaults. Field area assumes production runs for the stated months per year with the stated number of wheat harvests per year; it does not change what facilities demand per running month. Leaving run months, harvests or yield blank leaves only the field-area estimate uncalculated; everything else still renders. Line rules can differ per facility in FS25 — set them per building, not globally.

How the plan is calculated

The reference uses standard Bakery and Grain Mill entries in MrSNoopy1611's v1.4.3 sheet, not its separate shed variants. These are historical community records with no stated game build; compare your own facilities before planning a purchase.

The planner multiplies your monthly bread target by the flour ratio, then by the wheat ratio. Each facility's monthly capacity is divided between its lines only when the “share one monthly budget” rule is selected; with independent lines the recipe keeps its full capacity and the line count does not multiply it. Wheat demand becomes field area using your production run months per year, wheat harvests per year and yield per hectare per harvest.

To plan from your own save: enter the monthly output you want and the recipe's input per output, plus each facility's monthly output as observed or selected in your save. Then enter how many of each facility you already own — the plan compares the total facilities needed against what you have and shows the additional purchase gap.

Sources & data status

    Values on this page mix community references and illustrative sample assumptions; all of them remain labelled “not yet runtime-confirmed” until verified against the current build, and not every value has an independent source. Unconfirmed values are never shown as confirmed. Open “Advanced” above to check or replace any number before you trust a plan. No prices on purpose — this tool plans throughput, not profit.

    Seeing a different value in your own save? Send the number, your game version and what you observed via the contact page or the contribute page — reported values are verified before they replace a reference.

    Oil Mill capacity, recipe & optional field area

    Historical author sheet v1.4.3, row 40, standard Oil Mill (not shed). Recipe and capacity are references, not a current-build test. Sharing rule is an editable assumption; canola yield is your own input.

    No assumed crop yield. Without your yield, materials and facilities still calculate. Independent recipes keep the selected recipe’s capacity; selecting two recipes never doubles it.

    Plan canola supply and the extra Oil Mills you need

    Using the standard Oil Mill reference, 20 L canola makes 10 L oil, with 480 cycles per month: 4,800 L oil/month. A 6,000 L oil target needs 12,000 L canola and 2 Oil Mills in total. If you own 1, its monthly shortfall is 1,200 L oil: add 1 identical mill.

    If two recipes share that budget, each gets 2,400 L/month and this target needs 3 mills. If they run independently, this recipe stays at 4,800 L/month and needs 2 mills—not one. Set the rule to match your building. The sheet’s shed variant is 480 L/month and is not the reference used here.

    Greenhouse recipe & capacity

    Historical author sheet v1.4.3, Greenhouses tab, rows 6 / 15 / 24. Size changes output per cycle; these are not hydroponic or modded facilities. Replace the values if your save differs.

    Water demand is for your lettuce target, not every crop or full-speed production. Enter available water to subtract it for this one month; blank means no stock has been credited, while 0 means you have none. Trips round upward. Water is not assumed free or automatically supplied.

    Size the greenhouse and the water deliveries together

    The author sheet records lettuce at 1,008 / 2,016 / 4,032 L per month in small / medium / large greenhouses, at 2 L water per 1 L lettuce. A 6,000 L lettuce target needs 12,000 L water and 2 large greenhouses in total. With one large greenhouse owned, the lettuce capacity gap is 1,968 L/month: add one matching building.

    For an explicitly entered 5,000 L tanker with no water credited, that target takes at least 3 deliveries. With 3,000 L already available for this month, only 9,000 L remains to deliver: 2 trips. These are transport examples, not default vehicle or storage sizes. Two large greenhouses running the lettuce recipe at full speed instead produce 8,064 L lettuce and use 16,128 L water. Other active recipes are not included.

    Check the flour gap before buying a mill

    In the standard-facility reference from the author’s v1.4.3 sheet, a 5,400 L monthly bread target needs 10,800 L flour. One wheat mill supplies 10,080 L: a 720 L gap. This target needs 2 identical mills in total; if you own 1, add 1.

    This is not a universal or current-build guarantee and excludes the sheet’s separate shed variants. Recalculate using your facilities and check the original author’s data.
