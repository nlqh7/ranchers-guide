What to plant first

Your first field should be fast spring crops — turnips, lettuce, carrots — bought from Youssef, the seed vendor near the clothing store in Sunvale (look for the basket map icon). They mature before you can get lazy about watering, and the quick sell-replant loop compounds your starting cash. Tools and upgrades come from Lina's tool shop.

Once you can run two crop slots in parallel, split them: one fast slot compounding cash (carrots, onions) and one premium slot working on the big profit/day numbers (strawberries in spring, tomatoes and corn in summer, pumpkin and grape in fall).

Reading the table

  • Fast crops (3–5 days) dominate weeks one and two because they compound quickly and keep cash flowing.
  • Slow premium crops (8+ days) win once you have enough plots running in parallel — pumpkin, watermelon, and grape are the current leaders.
  • Regrowable crops (strawberry, tomato, cucumber, pepper, grape) only cost seed once, so their real profit/day climbs with every repeat harvest.
  • Season is law. Sort by season at the start of each in-game month and plant only what's in window — off-season planting is a total loss.
  • Greenhouse crops ignore seasons entirely but demand electricity and building investment first. Pineapple is the prize; it's a mid-game goal, not a starter.

Field mechanics that change the math

  • Water daily. Neglected crops die — a dead pumpkin is a 70 g hole, not a delay. Sprinklers (once you have electricity) remove the chore.
  • Scarecrows matter. Outdoor fields without scarecrow coverage take crow damage; factor replacement losses into tight margins.
  • Fertilizer at plant time from Youssef boosts the payout. We apply fertilizer to any crop with a base sell price above ~100 g.
  • Selling channels: town vendors buy basics instantly, while the auction market can fetch more from other players for bulk premium produce. Festival weeks can spike prices on specific crops — watch the calendar.
