How to build a trustworthy crop row

A useful crop row needs more than a screenshot of one price. Record the game version, season, seed source, seed cost, planting day, harvest day, yield per plot, sale destination, sale price, and any quality or processing modifier.

  • Use a control plot. Test one crop without fertilizer or processing before adding bonuses.
  • Measure occupied days. Count the days a plot cannot be replanted, not only the number printed on a seed description.
  • Separate sale channels. A local-market price and an auction result are different observations.
  • Repeat the test. One harvest can hide random yield or quality variation.
  • Keep the version. Early Access balance patches can make an old number accurate for one build and wrong for the next.

Sources and evidence policy

The confirmed rows come from the official Steam store page and official Steam updates, reviewed August 1, 2026. They describe systems, not a complete numerical crop catalog.

Community reports can point us toward a test, but they do not become database facts without a matching screenshot or repeatable in-game observation. A correction should include the game version and the screen or action that produced the value. Send evidence through the contact page.
