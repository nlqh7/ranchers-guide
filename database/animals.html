The confirmed ranch roster

Official Steam materials and the demo build confirm the following lineup for Early Access: hens (black, brown, and white variants) laying eggs in coops, roosters for fertilizing eggs and breeding, cattle producing high-value milk in barns, goats as a mid-tier dairy option, sheep producing wool for crafting chains, rabbits for compact fur herds, and horses for riding across Sunvale's 16 km² world. The developer's own framing: "From chickens and bunnies to goats and cows, you can pick your favorites or try to collect at least one of each."

How to use this table

  • Poultry first. Hens are the classic entry point: 250 g, a coop, and a daily egg means your money starts coming back inside a week.
  • Dairy is the mid-game engine. Goats and cows cost real money but pay steady, season-proof daily income — cows lead the table on raw profit/day.
  • Livestock with multi-day cycles (sheep, rabbits) smooth your schedule: fewer chores per day, decent lump sums per cycle.
  • Utility animals don't sell anything. A horse won't appear in your profit/day math, but the time it saves crossing the map pays for itself in extra working hours.

Unlock requirements at a glance

  • Coop animals (hens, roosters): build a small coop from the workbench crafting line — available in your first days.
  • Small barn animals (rabbits): a basic barn plus enough fence coverage.
  • Barn animals (sheep, goats, cows): the full barn, which wants cleared land, building materials, and a water source (well or water tower) within reach.
  • Horses: stable-style housing and a much bigger budget; treat as a mid-game mobility upgrade alongside the pickup and scooter.
