What is actually confirmed

The official Steam store description explicitly lists chickens, bunnies, goats, and cows. It also describes caring for animals as one part of the broader ranch loop. That supports the four roster rows above, but it does not support invented prices, product values, or rankings.

How to build a trustworthy animal row

  • Capture acquisition. Record seller, purchase price, prerequisites, and the game version.
  • Capture housing. Include structure cost, capacity, required utilities, and space.
  • Measure a full cycle. Track feed, water, care interactions, elapsed days, output quantity, and quality.
  • Test missed care. Do not assume the penalty; record what changes when an interaction is skipped.
  • Repeat before ranking. A profit comparison needs at least two consistent cycles and the same sale channel.

Questions still open in the current build

  • Are additional species present in the initial Early Access build?
  • Which animals use barns, coops, pasture, or another structure?
  • What daily care is mandatory, and what can be automated?
  • How do product quality, friendship, breeding, or seasons affect output?
  • Which costs are recurring, and how long does each investment take to repay?

Sources and evidence policy

The confirmed roster comes from the official Steam store page. Current transport, map, and probability rules come from official Steam updates, reviewed August 1, 2026.

Community reports can enter as labeled leads when their origin and build context are clear. They become corroborated or verified current-build values only after independent matching evidence. We will not add plausible genre-standard animals simply to make the table longer. Send a documented correction.
