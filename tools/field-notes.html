What makes a useful field note?

A useful record lets another player repeat the same action. Name the build, location, item quality, sale channel, game mode, quest step, and any condition that could change the result.

Good example

Observed once · build 0.8.10.455: sold one normal-quality item at the named market after recording the buy screen and sale screen. Needs a second observation before publication.

Weak example

“This crop is best.” It has no version, cost, yield, sale channel or reproducible comparison.
