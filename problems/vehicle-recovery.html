Your vehicle is stuck, broken or impounded

Official systemsBuild 0.8.10.455 baselineChecked August 2, 2026

Answer first

Use My Garage for an owned vehicle that is broken, destroyed, drowned or stuck. Repaired vehicles return through the nearest Car Pound parking location. A vehicle left outside your ranch or an approved Overnight Parking location can be impounded instead, and stolen vehicles have separate license-plate rules.

Choose the route by vehicle state

Owned and stuck, drowned, destroyed or broken

Open the in-game My Garage app and send the vehicle to a mechanic. The official system routes the repaired vehicle back through a Car Pound parking location.

Left in an unauthorized place

Check the Car Pound. Vehicles left outside your ranch and outside designated Overnight Parking can be impounded.

Currently drivable but damaged

A QuickFix Garage can repair vehicles, including during a police chase. Compare the immediate repair option with remote recovery before abandoning the vehicle.

Quest vehicle with an irreparable engine

The related quest can fail. Treat that as a quest state, then use the current replay behavior rather than trying to preserve the destroyed quest vehicle.

Situation → action decision table

Official Every route below comes from the official driving/My Garage update notes (0.8.10.420 era):

SituationWhat to do
Owned vehicle simply not where you left itOpen the My Garage phone app — it lists every vehicle you currently own, including ones seized for illegal parking.
Left outside your ranch or a designated Overnight Parking spotIt is impounded and transported to the Car Pound; manage and recover it through My Garage.
Broken, destroyed, drowned or stuckSend it to the mechanic from My Garage; the repaired vehicle is automatically returned to the nearest Car Pound parking location.
Stolen vehicleIt must receive new license plates at AutoHue before My Garage can manage it at all.
Stolen vehicle with forged plates, left outside safe parking againPolice can permanently seize it — the official notes say there is no way to recover it.
Long quest drive you do not want to doOn selected quests, use Skip Quest Driving: your vehicle and all passengers (players, NPCs and farm animals) are transported directly to the destination.

Avoiding impound

The official transport update identifies two safe categories for leaving a vehicle:

  • Your ranch.
  • One of the 17 designated Overnight Parking locations.

Ordinary roadside space is not automatically overnight-safe. When testing a location, look for the designated parking context instead of assuming any open bay is valid. If a vehicle disappears after being left overnight, check impound before treating it as a save loss.

Official "Inside your gates" is the operative rule for the ranch option. In a Steam discussion (August 5, 2026), moderator Jennoula confirmed: "Vehicles have to be parked inside your gates not out on city property." Parking on municipal ground or just outside your gate still counts as unauthorized and the vehicle can be towed.

Stolen vehicle rules

Ownership and license status change the recovery outcome:

Vehicle stateOfficially described outcome
Stolen, original plateIt must receive new license plates at AutoHue before My Garage can manage it.
Stolen, forged plate, parked safelyManagement depends on the completed plate change and current ownership state.
Stolen, forged plate, left outside safe parkingPolice can seize it permanently; the official notes say it cannot be recovered.

Because permanent seizure exists, do not test parking rules with a vehicle you cannot afford to lose.

If My Garage does not show the vehicle

Record whether it was purchased or stolen, whether the plate was changed, the last parking location, the game build and whether a day transition occurred. Those facts are more useful than repeatedly reloading. Submit a report only after checking the Car Pound and the ownership rules above.

Arrested or jailed? Your vehicle may have been impounded

Player-tested A vehicle that disappears after an arrest is usually not lost to a bug — it can be impounded while you are in jail, and it comes back through the phone. Player shredmentalism reported a 144,000-credit car missing after release from jail, then found it through the garage section on the phone and recovered it (Steam discussion, around August 6, 2026). Moderator Jennoula confirmed the intended route by pointing to the Mechanic app. Independently, loridhm recovered a lost car the same way via the My Garage phone app on August 3 (Steam discussion). Before assuming a save loss after an arrest, check My Garage and the Mechanic app first.

Vehicles exploding nearby is intended, not a bug

Official Dismantling or destroying objects near a vehicle can set off an explosion that sends you to the hospital and costs you items. Moderator Jennoula confirmed "this is an intended mechanic" after player gamingmarcel20 was caught by it twice (Steam discussion, August 5, 2026). Our practical advice: drive your vehicle away before dismantling buildings, crates or other objects near it.

Selling vehicles is not in the game yet

Official There is currently no way to sell a vehicle — including a stolen one with new paint and plates. Moderator Jennoula stated the option "is not currently in the game but it is planned" (Steam discussion, August 2, 2026). Until it ships, treat every vehicle purchase or theft as a permanent addition to your garage.

Evidence and limits

The recovery destinations, 17 Overnight Parking locations, My Garage conditions and stolen-vehicle plate rules come from official Steam updates. Exact repair prices, delays and every Car Pound position are not claimed here because they still need current-build observation.
