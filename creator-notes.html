Passing out and the day counter

Source entry

Source: days lose when pass out? · Reviewed: 2026-08-13 · Build scope: Early Access discussion; exact patch not stated

What it helps with

Moderator Jennoula explains that when the clock has passed midnight, sleeping or passing out advances the date again on wake-up: the day that began before midnight becomes the next day after midnight, then the following day after sleep/pass-out. The moderator says the game does not make this clear and the behavior was flagged for review.

How this site uses it

Add a clear calendar warning to the farming and beginner guides: stop before midnight when a crop deadline matters, and distinguish a normal sleep from a post-midnight sleep/pass-out. Do not use the thread to infer crop growth timing or universal crop-loss behavior.

Use risk

The thread does not state a current build number and contains a separate unresolved crop-growth report. Only the date-counter explanation is treated as an official answer; the crop-loss symptom remains unverified.
