Creator Notes

These notes explain what other creators and reference sources help us check, and what cannot be treated as a current-build answer. The site adds summaries, comparisons and evidence gates; it does not copy prose, images or video.

Editorial boundary: Build before conclusion: every source has a date and scope, and an older lead does not become a current mechanic automatically.

Games Station

Source entry

Source: Games Station current-build gameplay archive · Reviewed: 2026-08-07 · Build scope: 0.8.10.455

What it helps with

A current-build walkthrough used for bounded observations of animals, crops, shops, NPC conversations and map recognition. The archive is a visual reference, not a replacement for an in-game test.

How this site uses it

We cite the relevant timestamp or use it to cross-check a page claim, then keep the observation tied to 0.8.10.455. We do not reproduce the creator's script, thumbnail or footage.

Use risk

The game has moved beyond this launch-build archive. Prices, prompts and bug behavior may have changed in later builds.

Steam Community Guides

Source entry

Source: The Ranchers Steam Community Guides · Reviewed: 2026-08-21 · Build scope: Current index; individual guides vary

What it helps with

A useful place to find player questions and test leads about shop hours, seed yields and animal production. The index does not make every guide authoritative.

How this site uses it

We use a guide as a lead only when its version, steps and raw observation are visible. A claim enters a current answer only after an official response, a second independent record or a reproducible test supports it.

Use risk

Older guides can preserve Alpha-era values or conclusions. Check the build before using any number, opening hour or production rule.

The Ranchers Official Wiki

Source entry

Source: The Ranchers official Wiki · Reviewed: 2026-08-21 · Build scope: Alpha-era pages; current status varies

What it helps with

A taxonomy and visual checklist for buildings, animals, items and places. It helps identify what to investigate, especially when a page names an entity or shows a building view.

How this site uses it

We use names and relationships as research leads, then keep current-build facts in our own evidence records. Historical values are not copied into current tables.

Use risk

Many pages retain pre-EA or Alpha-era values, and an image or category does not prove a current coordinate, recipe or unlock condition.
