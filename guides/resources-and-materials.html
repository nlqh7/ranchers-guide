Stone: gather your owned supply first

Current-build reports describe loose Stone on the player's ranch land and say the visible supply can be exhausted. That makes it useful for the first shelter and workbench decisions, but not a promise of an infinite source.

  1. Collect visible loose Stone on land you own.
  2. Keep the amount needed for early construction instead of spending it on every optional placement.
  3. When the visible supply is gone, stop searching for a mine as though one is already available.

Risk: a current discussion reports that taking resources from land you do not own can trigger the crime system. Treat neighboring plots as unsafe until your own save proves otherwise. A reliable repeatable Stone route and mine availability remain open questions.

Sources: official mine availability answer · current Stone and crime discussion

Wood Logs: useful early, but respawn is not established

Community notes point to cutting trees as the current way to obtain Wood Logs. The retained evidence does not establish how quickly trees return or how ownership affects gathering, so use this as a practical starting route rather than a complete economy table.

The launch-build footage opened one Red Tent recipe at 8 Stone + 10 Wood Logs. That is a useful first construction reserve, not evidence for every shelter or furniture recipe.

Hay: feed is confirmed, gathering is not

An official moderator answer identifies Hay as chicken feed. Put it in the interior coop trough, or load a connected silo when you are testing automation. This is the part you can act on now.

A single current-build report suggests cutting bushes or searching crates and barrels on NPC farms. It is useful as a test route, but it is not a guaranteed drop table. Record the location, container, build and whether the item was actually Hay before treating it as corroborated.

Charcoal: a lead to test, not a recipe

A community scan describes a possible loop of burning Wood Logs, waiting for a material change, then using a fire extinguisher before collecting Charcoal. The retained record has no direct public post URL and no current screenshot, so this page does not present it as a confirmed recipe.

If you test it, use one log first. Record the exact object, ignition method, elapsed time, fire state, extinguisher action and resulting inventory. Do not burn a full reserve based on the old Alpha context; the historical moderator answer only confirms the extinguisher as the intended way to put out fires.

Zirconite: buy it before searching for a mine

The official moderator answer says purchasing Zirconite is the only current route until mines are implemented. Multiple community answers identify Meriam at City Hall as the seller, but the retained evidence does not support a stable current price.

  1. Go to City Hall on the map.
  2. Find Meriam and check the current materials or blueprint-related purchase list.
  3. Use the exact price shown in your save; do not replace it with an Alpha-era table.

What to record when testing a resource route

A useful resource report needs more than “I found it.” Record the named build, platform, owned or unowned land, season or day, exact object searched, quantity before and after, and a screenshot of the inventory or shop screen. If another player can repeat the same result under comparable conditions, the site can promote the lead from a report to a corroborated finding.

Resource FAQ

Can I mine Stone or Zirconite right now?

Do not assume it. The current official answer says mines are not available in the reviewed build; Stone should start with loose pieces on owned land, while Zirconite should be purchased.

Is the Charcoal burning method confirmed?

No. It is a community lead with historical fire-extinguisher context. Test it with one log and record the result before relying on it.
