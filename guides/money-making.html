The Ranchers: Selling Crops & Getting Paid

Updated September 12, 2026 · CashIn, payment checks and early spending

Harvest ready? Choose by when you need the money

Use CashIn if you can wait until the next day. If you need cash for supplies sooner, check Leafy Market. Keep materials for your active quest and next build before depositing a whole stack.

Already deposited? Go straight to payment checks. Still saving for your first setup? Use the early-money plan.

CashIn or Leafy Market? Decide by payment timing

A moderator answer on August 12 says prices were the same, but CashIn required an overnight wait; the market was better for urgent cash.

That is a dated answer, not a complete price table for every later patch.

  • You can wait: use CashIn and follow the next-day payment steps below.
  • You need supplies sooner: go to Leafy Market and check its sell menu and offer before confirming the trade.

When the prices match, choose by payment timing and travel. There is no reason to haul a harvest into town solely because you assume the market pays a premium.

CashIn: how to sell and get paid next day

Short answer: Move the items you want to sell into the CashIn box. Deposits pay out the next day, and better-quality items pay more.

  1. Choose the amount: Transfer a full stack, or use Take 1 item when you want to deposit a single unit.
  2. Put it in CashIn: Drag the selected items into the box with mouse controls, or use Move with controller controls.
  3. Wait for payment: The deposit does not pay immediately. CashIn Corp pays it out the next day.
  4. Compare like with like: Quality affects the payout: better quality means higher pay.

An older settlement-screen observation showed the eventual crop payout as one combined Farming total, so it cannot establish a per-crop sell price.

Location: Open the Cash-In box on the map.

Game files The source does not provide an accepted-item list, exact item prices, payout time of day, fees, or a numerical quality multiplier. Checked against 0.8.10.842.

Deposited items but cannot see the money?

Check the stage of the sale before treating it as a lost payment. These checks follow the in-game tutorial and the recorded settlement screen.

What you seeWhat to check next
The items are still in your bag.Reopen CashIn and confirm that you transferred them into the box. Mouse controls use drag; controller controls use Move.
Your balance did not rise when you deposited.The tutorial says payment is next day. An unchanged balance at the moment of deposit does not show that the sale failed.
A Farming amount appears, but not the crop's name.The recorded statement groups crop income into Farming. Look at that total; do not expect it to list each crop separately.
Your balance rose by less than the income line.Also check Water, Energy and personal bills on the statement. Crop revenue and the final balance change are different amounts.
The next day has passed and the expected payment is still missing.Keep the deposit item, quantity, quality, date and next statement. Include them in a bug report; the retained sources do not establish a universal fix.

A recorded sale: from deposit to Farming income

Games Station's playthrough shows both ends of the flow in build 0.8.10.455. The timestamps let you inspect the deposit and the later statement directly.

  1. Deposit — 59:41–59:57. The player opens the CashIn Export Storage Chest and moves produce into it.
  2. Statement — 76:14–76:24. The Day 8 screen records Farming +1,534C as a combined category total.

Use this as a payment example, not a crop price. The statement does not identify the quantity and value of each crop. Dividing 1,534C by a guessed harvest count would give an unsupported sell price.

The 1,440C strawberry-seed purchase elsewhere in the video is a separate observation. It cannot be subtracted from this combined Farming total to calculate strawberry profit.

Spend from the payment you received

  1. Repeat your existing cycle. Set aside the next seeds, feed and known bills first.
  2. Protect the next task. Check the required materials before selling stored supplies or buying an optional machine.
  3. Expand with the remainder. Choose one improvement you can afford to run, including its ongoing costs.

Budget example, using invented round numbers: if you have 1,000C and the next cycle needs 600C, only 400C remains before other bills or purchases. This illustrates the method; it is not a game price.

Before buying more seed

Read the seed's season and growth information, then check the crop database. A store listing tells you the cost to buy seed; it does not tell you what the harvest will sell for.

For a regrowing crop, count only harvests that fit the time available. The recorded strawberry tooltip says 7 days to first growth and 3 days between later harvests; it does not guarantee a full season's yield.

Other selling routes: what is actually established?

Auction market: the September FAQ supersedes old auction-market promises. This page cannot recommend it as a currently available selling route.

Vendor menus: the video shows Meriam's “Sell items” option, but does not use it. That does not establish accepted goods or a better price than CashIn.

Selling surplus electricity

The current FAQ confirms surplus resource sales. Exact electricity rates and payback are not established here; use the power guide for contracts and equipment.

Avoid counting the same energy twice: power used on the ranch can reduce a bill, while power sold earns revenue. A smaller bill is not a second sale.

Fishing and vehicle sales

Fishing is excluded from the early-money plan: the August 1 moderator answer says it was not yet available. A coastline screenshot or ledger label cannot prove otherwise.

The retained August 2 moderator answer says vehicles cannot be sold, including stolen ones. Do not plan on car resale income; see the vehicle-selling answer.

Helping a friend: who keeps the earnings?

The September FAQ says guests keep their money, XP, inventory items and blueprints. World and quest progress stays with the host, who must be online.

Agree who buys supplies for the host's ranch. The FAQ does not establish how a guest's CashIn deposit is assigned; do not assume that particular payment is shared. See the co-op guide.

Compare profit once you have a sale value

A sale total is revenue. Subtract what that cycle consumed before comparing it with another activity.

  • Crop cycle: total harvest sales − seeds − consumable supplies and attributable bills.
  • Animal cycle: product sales − feed and recurring care; account for purchase and housing separately.
  • Upgrade payback: setup cost ÷ additional net income per day. No positive extra income means this formula gives no finite payback.

Divide the cycle's net amount by elapsed game days to compare daily returns. For regrowing crops, total all included harvests and charge the seed purchase once.

Use the profit calculator when you have those inputs. An unknown sale value should stay unknown, rather than become a zero or a shop purchase price.

What should I record for a fair comparison?

Record build, item, quantity, quality, input cost, planting or purchase date, harvest dates, and the actual payment. Use the same conditions for the comparison.

Keep travel and active work separate from growing time. If a deposit mixes several products, keep its combined income rather than inventing individual prices.

Quick questions

Can I sell one item without depositing the whole stack?

Yes. The CashIn tutorial includes Take 1 item. Select that quantity, then drag it into the box or use Move with controller controls.

Does higher quality pay more?

The CashIn tutorial says better quality gives higher pay. It does not supply an exact multiplier or a complete price list.

Is strawberry the most profitable crop?

That is not established. The recorded seed price and regrowth interval do not provide harvest yield or sale value, both of which are needed to calculate profit.
