The crafting flow: plan first, materials second

Crafting questions are easiest to solve when the plan and the output are recorded together. Follow this order instead of farming every material before checking the recipe:

  1. Open the plan list. Check the phone crafting app or workbench in your current save and note the exact plan name.
  2. Read the requirement screen. Record each material and quantity shown. Do not substitute an Alpha-era table for the screen in front of you.
  3. Gather only the bottleneck. Start with the material the plan actually asks for; the resource guide explains which routes are current, reported or unknown.
  4. Craft the item. If the plan is not visible, record the locked label or the quest step instead of assuming the recipe was removed.
  5. Place and test it. A successful craft does not prove that a building part satisfies a quest objective. For that separate question, use the building and placement guide.

When crafting or placement fails

Crafting FAQ

The plan is listed but I cannot craft it

Record the exact plan name, missing material label, app or workbench used, build number and whether the item is locked or simply unaffordable. Then compare the current screen with the evidence in the research queue. Do not call it a bug from one missing recipe.

The item crafted but the quest did not advance

That is a placement/objective problem, not necessarily a crafting problem. Keep the quest text and blueprint name, then use the roof objective decision guide or placement reports.

Can I trust an old crafting table from the Alpha?

No. Older official and community material is useful for taxonomy and historical context, but recipe quantities and unlock timing need a current-build screen. The site keeps historical values labeled instead of silently promoting them.

What to record for a useful crafting report

  • Build and platform: the title-screen version and whether the test was solo or co-op.
  • Interface path: phone app name, workbench type and the exact menu tab.
  • Plan screen: full plan title, requirements, locked state and selected inputs.
  • Result: the crafted item, inventory change, placement result and quest state.
  • Evidence: one screenshot before and after, or a short clip with the version visible.

Send a documented correction through Contribute. A single report stays a lead; two independent current-build records under comparable conditions can promote it to a corroborated finding.
