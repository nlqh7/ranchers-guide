The Ranchers Controls & Camera Settings Guide

Camera update reviewed September 19, 2026 · 0.8.10.871

Current camera controls come from the September 16 official update. Older general key mappings remain labeled with their original source date.

Fix the view first

Double-press C to reset direction without changing zoom. Use V or double-press the right mouse button to reset both. For a camera that keeps turning back, adjust the walking and driving follow settings below.

Choose the setting for your problem

Reset direction only: double-press C. Reset direction and zoom: press V or double-press the right mouse button. Both work with auto-follow off.

Building controls

Official (Feb 2026 update, demo-era); recheck against the current EA build. Two options were added for builders:

  • Auto Equip Hammer after placement — default ON. The Hammer stays in your hands after placing a blueprint, so chaining multiple placements does not require re-equipping.
  • Auto Re-Equip Same Blueprint after placement — default OFF. Enable this if you are placing many copies of the same piece.

The same update added clearer error messages when placing blueprints — for unpurchased farm parcels, unauthorized zones or interiors, collisions or failed placement requirements, and exceeding City Hall building height limits. If placement fails, read the message before assuming a bug; see the Building & Placement Guide and the roof quest troubleshooting page for the most common blockers.

Make the camera more comfortable

Try one change at a time: disable unwanted auto-follow, or adjust its delay if you want following with less frequent realignment. Then tune mouse sensitivity on the same short walking or driving route.

These are comfort suggestions, not a guaranteed solution. Take a break if movement causes discomfort.

Controls FAQ

How do I rotate the camera in The Ranchers?

Hold the Right Mouse Button, per the official control simplification update (Feb 2026, demo-era — recheck against the current EA build). All primary tool actions moved to the Left Mouse Button at the same time.

Where are the camera settings?

Open the menu with ESC — Game Settings are accessible directly from the ESC menu — and look for the dedicated Camera Settings section added in the Feb 2026 update (demo-era; recheck against the current EA build).

Does the game pause when I open Settings?

In solo mode, yes — the official notes say the game pauses while you use the Feedback App or Settings App (Feb 2026 update, demo-era; recheck against the current EA build). Do not rely on pausing in a co-op session.

Is there QWERTZ keyboard support?

Yes — QWERTZ layout support (Germany, Austria, etc.) was added in the official Feb 2026 update (demo-era; recheck against the current EA build).

Can I remap keyboard keys?

Current status: No current source confirms keyboard remapping in the current build. In a July 31 reply, moderator Jennoula said key mapping was planned but did not make that Early Access build. The September developer FAQ confirms mouse-and-keyboard and controller support, but does not confirm remapping; the 0.8.10.871 update lists camera changes, not key remapping. Open Settings from the ESC menu and check whether your build has a key-mapping option. If it does not, do not treat the planned feature as available; the official sources checked here do not give an in-game remapping workaround.

July 31 moderator reply on key mapping · September developer FAQ · 0.8.10.871 camera update

The camera keeps swinging back while I drive. Can I stop it?

Check Automatically Follow Vehicle Direction, which defaults to on. Disable it for manual control, or adjust Vehicle - Camera Follow Delay. See the current settings table.
