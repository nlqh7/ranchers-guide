Roof Quest Stuck in The Ranchers? Building Quest Fixes That Work

Updated for Early Access 0.8.10.x (August 2026) · August 7, 2026 · Reading time: ~7 min

Evidence status: 1 officially confirmed known issue · 2 multi-player corroborated fixes · 2 single reports (labeled) · reviewed Aug 7, 2026 — How we verify →

If your roof quest will not complete after you built the roof, you are not alone — this was one of the most-reported problems on the Steam discussion board in the first week of August 2026, with players posting about it in English, German and Russian, and some getting stuck even earlier at the first step of placing stairs (Tutorial build roof, Aug 4; Whitebear, Aug 2; Campingqueen769, Aug 3).

Answer first

The quest tracker getting stuck is an officially confirmed known issue Official. Do these three things in order:

  1. Press T and check the quest journal on your phone — the next objective may already be listed there even though the on-screen tracker looks stuck, and you can keep playing.
  2. Sleep to the next day.
  3. Restart the game client (save first, then fully quit and relaunch).

If the real problem is that you cannot find the roof, door or bed blueprints at the workbench, that is a different issue — a scrolling UI quirk — covered in Symptom B below.

Symptom A: roof built, quest will not complete (tracker stuck)

Official Corroborated by players

This is the officially confirmed case. Moderator/support Jennoula confirmed in "Tutorial build roof" (Aug 4, 2026) that "there is a known issue with the quest tracker sometimes getting stuck," and gave the workarounds that have worked for almost every player so far:

  1. Press T and see whether your next objective shows up in the phone quest journal. The on-screen tracker may still look stuck, but you can continue the quest anyway.
  2. Sleep to the next day — this sometimes gets the tracker moving again.
  3. Restart the game client.

The developers are still investigating why the tracker gets stuck, but you should be able to continue in the meantime.

Two independent players corroborate the restart/logout route in "Cant finish Roof quest...?" (Aug 3): X-C0brA-X saved, restarted the game, and the quest showed as finished after the restart; KnottyBoy did the quest steps via the phone journal, then logged totally out and back in, after which the on-screen quest passed.

Symptom B: no roof, door or bed blueprint at the workbench (UI scrolling)

Corroborated by players

Before assuming your game is broken, check how far you scrolled. In "Missing roof" (Aug 2), SUwUMaBUwU pointed out that roofs and beds sit far to the right in the building tab list — scroll slowly with the mouse wheel, because scrolling too far flips you over to the furniture tab. Minay, who had restarted and even started a new save without success, confirmed that scrolling slowly with the mouse wheel revealed the roofs and beds.

A second, independent group reported the same class of problem for door blueprints in "No door plan at workbench" (Aug 4): rllewis3 found that workbench pages can be dragged back and forth with the left mouse button, which revealed plans that seemed missing; jw62157 (the thread author) realized that clicking the arrow keys at the top slides the choices over but often hides some blueprints behind the left panel that keeps opening.

Practical takeaway: use slow mouse-wheel scrolling or left-mouse dragging to page the workbench list, and be careful with the top arrow buttons — they can leave blueprints hidden behind the side panel.

Symptom C: cannot build a door

Single report

One player, Earth Hunter (in "No door plan at workbench", Aug 4), reports that the blueprint list contains two door types — an exterior door and an interior door — and that you also need a wall with a door opening for the door to fit. This is a single community report, not officially confirmed, but it matches the building system's wall-cutout logic and is worth checking before reporting a bug.

Symptom D: hole in the middle of the roof (Roof Cap Lvl 2)

Single report unverified

Several players use Roof Cap Lvl 2 to fill the hole in the middle and then wonder whether that counts. One player, bucfanjoe (in "Cant finish Roof quest...?", Aug 3), reports that their own build moved past the roof step even with a hole in the middle, as long as the exterior edges had a roof tile — but notes being unsure whether Lvl 2 caps count, and adds that having actual doors and windows placed in the wall cutouts may matter. Treat this as a single unverified report: if Symptom A's fixes fail, completing the wall openings and edging the roof is a reasonable next test, not a proven fix.

Troubleshooting flow (our consolidated checklist)

Work through these in order — each step rules out one of the symptoms above:

  1. Can you find the blueprint at all? If no roof/door/bed plan appears at the workbench, fix the UI first: slow mouse-wheel scroll, left-mouse drag, avoid the top arrows hiding plans behind the side panel (Symptom B).
  2. Is the building actually complete? Walls placed, wall with a door opening for the door, exterior and interior door types distinguished (Symptom C), roof tiles on all exterior edges (Symptom D).
  3. Is the tracker stuck? Building done but the quest will not advance → press T and check the phone quest journal, sleep to the next day, then restart the client (Symptom A, officially confirmed known issue).
  4. Still stuck after all three? Report it on the Steam discussion board with your build version and screenshots — the developers are actively investigating the tracker issue.

Roof quest FAQ

I built the roof exactly as shown. Why won't the quest complete?

Most likely the quest tracker is stuck — an officially confirmed known issue in Early Access. Press T to check the phone quest journal (the next step may already be there), sleep to the next day, or restart the client. These workarounds have worked for almost every player so far, per the support team.

There are no roof or bed blueprints in my workbench. Is my save broken?

Probably not. Multiple players "missing" roofs, beds and doors were actually hitting a scrolling UI quirk: the plans sit far right in the building tab, fast scrolling overshoots into the furniture tab, and the top arrow buttons can hide plans behind the left panel. Scroll slowly with the mouse wheel or drag the pages with the left mouse button.

Does a Roof Cap Lvl 2 hole in the middle block the quest?

Unknown. One player reports progressing with a hole in the middle as long as exterior edges had roof tiles, but was unsure whether Lvl 2 caps count — a single unverified report. If the official tracker fixes fail, edging the roof and fitting doors/windows into wall cutouts is a sensible next test.
