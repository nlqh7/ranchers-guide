Starting your first plantation with the hoe

Short answer: Till land you own when the hoe square is white, then plant a seed and water it. Keep the season right, water every day, remove weeds and stop birds from stealing seeds.

  1. Find a valid spot: Move onto land you own. A red hoe-placement square means an obstacle blocks the spot; white means it is ready.
  2. Prepare the soil: When the placement square is white, use the hoe to till the ground.
  3. Plant and water: Equip a seed, stand over the tilled soil, press the use button, then water it with the watering can.
  4. Maintain the plot: Use the correct season, water every day, remove weeds from existing plantations with the hoe, and keep birds from stealing the seeds.

Game files The source does not state growth times, what one missed watering day does, how to obtain the tools, which crops belong to each season, how to stop birds, crop yield, or profit. Checked against 0.8.10.842.

Why did crops disappear or lose quality?

Check the End of Day report and the affected plantation's info popup. Match the exact warning to the next step instead of guessing from the season or assuming a save bug.

WarningNext step
Birds ate seedsPlace a scarecrow.
Birds ate some producePlace a scarecrow to protect the plantation.
Season change destroyed the plantationCheck the crop's growing season before replanting.
Plantation dried out from lack of waterRestore watering and keep the plantation watered.
Weeds lowered produce qualityRemove weeds regularly.
Lack of water lowered produce qualityRestore watering; this is separate from a plantation drying out.

The info popup also shows the seed name, growing season, growth time and whether the crop supports repeat harvests. The Harvest button appears only when produce is ready.

September 14 official update, 0.8.10.868. These warnings help diagnose the cause; they do not guarantee recovery of lost crops.

Can I leave ripe crops unharvested?

The December 2025 update said ripe produce would not be destroyed merely because you forgot to harvest. That is historical guidance, not protection against other damage.

Missing seeds need a separate check. The September plantation update confirms that birds can eat seeds or produce, and that season changes and lack of water can destroy plantations.

Read the loss warning before replanting. The older statement that seeds disappear only when the plant dies is not a complete rule for the current version.

Season changes can destroy crops: check the growing season

Official The September update confirms season-change crop loss. Compare the calendar with the growing season in the plantation popup before planting.

Two August 2026 threads reported crops disappearing around a season boundary, but they do not independently establish every calendar detail. In "WTF ?? A whole field of eggplants gone after sleeping ???" (Aug 3, 2026), the poster lost an entire eggplant field overnight; player Mancer explained that day 30 is the last harvest day, day 1 begins the new season, and seasonal crops are removed during the rollover. FIDELIS reported disappearing eggplants in "Things disappear" (Aug 6), but that report alone does not verify Mancer's exact day-30 cutoff.

Mancer's season-seed list from the same thread is a single community report, so treat it as a working reference, not gospel: summer — green lettuce, eggplant, corn; spring — strawberries, garlic, green salad; autumn — onions, carrots, pumpkins. Our own video-observed data confirms strawberry as a spring seed and red lettuce with a spring tag on build 0.8.10.455 (see the crop database).

Practical takeaway: check the calendar before a big planting run late in a season — seeds planted on day 28 of a season may never reach their first harvest.

Regrow vs one-time crops

Video-observed recorded on build 0.8.10.455

Retained gameplay footage of the launch build shows the two crop archetypes side by side:

  • Strawberry Seeds (144C): spring seed, first harvest 7 days after planting, then re-harvests every 3 days — a recurring crop.
  • Red Lettuce Seeds (48C): spring seed, matures in 3 days with no regrow line on the seed description — a one-time crop you replant.

The official pre-EA update's "simpler re-grow flow for recurring crops" Official matches this split: recurring crops keep their plant and cycle again, one-time crops are replanted from new seeds. Exact regrow intervals for other crops are still being collected — the crop database tracks each one with its own evidence label.

Removing plots and watering crops

To remove a plot: equip the hoe and use its plantation-removal action. Each removal deducts 2 Nature Skill Points. Check the target first; removal is not weeding.

To keep a crop: follow the planting tutorial and water daily. Find sprinkler materials in the equipment table; its configuration ranges are not measured plot coverage.

Why do older guides say to wait for dry days?

That was the July–August moderator workaround for empty plots. Hoe removal now provides a direct option. The old drying advice was never a watering schedule for planted crops.

September 14 plantation-removal update.

Selling your harvest

Video-observed recorded on build 0.8.10.455

Harvests are sold through the CashIn chest: deposit produce during the day and collect the payment the next day. The older settlement recording grouped crop income under one "Farming" line and did not break down individual crop prices. See the money-making guide's CashIn section for the complete current route.

The Dec 2, 2025 update also mentions an economy pass with fruit, vegetable and seed prices adjusted Official pre-EA, which is why we never quote Alpha-era price lists as current values. Per-crop sell prices remain unknown while the settlement screen stays aggregated — see the crop database for what is measured versus pending.

Fertilizer: official warning, current behavior pending

Official warning issued Aug 4, 2026; latest-build behavior not re-tested

In "Furtilizer not worth it" (Aug 3, 2026), a player documented spending 8,000C on legendary fertilizer that covered 8 of 24 plots and netted only 79C of extra profit. Support moderator Jennoula replied on Aug 4, 2026: "we are aware that the fertilizer is not currently calculating correctly and it is being looked into, so for now just avoid investing in fertilizer until we get it adjusted again."

The 0.8.10.842 hotfix notes (Aug 21, 2026) do not announce a fertilizer fix, but omission from patch notes is not proof that behavior stayed unchanged. We have not re-tested fertilizer on the latest build. Until a current-build test or official reply confirms the calculation was adjusted, the cautious recommendation is to avoid a large fertilizer investment. Fertilizer is still visible in the shop (Leafy Market's Seed & Fertilizer tab, video-observed on 0.8.10.455) — availability does not verify its return.

What is the greenhouse and how do you unlock it? (Hold)

Historical Single report

The greenhouse appears in official materials as one of the ways to build out the farm, and one August 2026 player (Mancer, in the eggplant thread) reports that greenhouses allow out-of-season planting, saying they tested planting in winter with one — a single self-reported test, not yet corroborated. The only unlock-path evidence on record is from November 2022: the developers confirmed back then that the greenhouse blueprint was sold by Lina and required both materials and money. Whether that vendor, price and requirement survived into Early Access is unknown.

Farming FAQ

Do I have to water crops every day?

Yes—the current-build tutorial tells players to water planted seeds every day. It does not state the exact penalty or recovery after one missed day. The separate “few dry days” rule applies only to empty, unseeded tilled plots.

What happens if I forget to harvest?

The December 2025 notes said missed harvesting alone does not destroy ripe produce. The September 2026 update separately confirms bird, season and water-related losses. Read the plantation warning; an unharvested crop is not protected from these causes.

Is fertilizer worth buying?

The Aug 4 support warning said fertilizer was calculating incorrectly and advised players to avoid investing until it was adjusted. The August notes did not announce a fix; the latest-build return has not been measured here. Avoid a large purchase until current evidence confirms its return.

How do I make a new field, and how do I get a greenhouse?

Follow the hoe tutorial: find a white valid placement square on land you own, till, plant and water. Greenhouse unlocking is a separate question; the older Lina blueprint lead still needs verification.
