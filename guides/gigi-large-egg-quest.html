Real Eggs, Real Evidence: Gigi Quest Walkthrough (The Ranchers)

Updated for Early Access 0.8.10.x (August 2026) · August 7, 2026 · Reading time: ~8 min

Evidence status: 4 player-corroborated findings · 1 detailed single report (labeled) · 1 conflicting/unknown item · reviewed Aug 7, 2026 — How we verify →

Answer first

One-sentence version of the quest: pet your chickens daily until their rating rises and they lay 2 large eggs, manually save, then take the subway — not a car — to hand the eggs over, escape the police on foot, and follow the mini-map star marker all the way to Gigi's front door to finish.

The two most common failure points: players never get large eggs because they do not raise the chickens' rating (step 1), and the final hand-in bugs out if you drive or if Gigi will not leave your car (step 4). Yes, the quest is completable in Early Access — and no, Gigi not contacting you afterwards matches what other players see — multiple players report no further quest has appeared in the current build (details).

Step 1: getting 2 large eggs

Corroborated by players

The quest needs 2 large eggs, and regular eggs do not count — Bobthekiller's exact blocker in "Real eggs, real evidence" (Aug 4, 2026) was only ever receiving regular eggs. DJM's answer in the same thread: pet the chickens every day; when their rating goes up, they start laying larger eggs, and once you have 2, the quest shows you where to take them. So if you never see large eggs, the fix is daily petting to raise the rating, not more hens.

Keeping your chickens alive (the prerequisite)

Corroborated by players

Large eggs require living, cared-for chickens — and one player (minximoo24, "Animals keep dying", Aug 5) lost 12 chickens in a row despite having food and water. Two independent players (Fight or Flight Gamer and maxitubby) give the same survival checklist:

  • Buy a heater — from the girl who sells you the chickens, not the one Meriam sells at City Hall.
  • Food and water inside and outside the barn.
  • Deposit chicks inside the barn, not in the enclosure.
  • Sickness → vet immediately. Watch their health daily.

Chicken coop delivery bug (know before you stock the coop)

Single report

Big Grizzly reports a detailed coop bug in "A temp chicken issue fix!" (Aug 4): if you submit chickens at the coop door (open or not), they enter the coop's stock count but disappear — or end up stuck "inside the coop but on the outside." You must physically carry and deposit them inside the coop. If chickens already vanished this way, the reported recovery is: remove all remaining chickens, demolish the coop — the lost chickens reappear on the grass — then rebuild and place them back inside manually. This is a single but detailed player report; treat it as the best current workaround, not confirmed behavior.

Step 2–3: handing over the eggs and escaping the police

Corroborated by players

Handing over the eggs triggers a police chase, and three independent players give the same advice: take the subway to the hand-in, do not drive (Seansniper27 — "went in by subway and not car, found out the hard way"; bucfanjoe; Fight or Flight Gamer). A car chase with the starter vehicle is where most failed attempts happen.

For escaping on foot, bucfanjoe describes a route that worked ("Large Egg Quest Gone", Aug 3): run for the river — it usually shakes the drones — then continue under the ferris wheel and up toward the museum until the police lose you, then let Gigi catch up. Seansniper27 adds that the next objective can point you to her house, back to the hand-in spot, or somewhere in between — watch the tracker rather than assuming one fixed destination.

Stuck point: Gigi keeps following you and will not leave

Partially corroborated workaround

The most-reported blocker after the chase: the quest says "take Gigi home," but she keeps following you and you cannot drop her off (meijvistom, "Real Eggs, Real Evidence", Aug 2 — reloading and restarting did not help them). WildW0lf, whose car was impounded mid-quest, could never get Gigi back into a car and had to abandon the quest.

One detailed working route comes from Fight or Flight Gamer (in this thread): after the on-foot escape, talking to Gigi only repeated her egg line — but the mini-map showed a mission star marker that the big map did not. Following the mini-map star on foot, with Gigi following, led to her house — and only once at her front door did she become available to talk to, which cleared the quest. Our honest summary: this worked for at least one player but reloads do not reliably fix the stuck state for everyone, so prevent it instead — go by subway, stay on foot, and keep Gigi following you from the escape to her door.

If the quest disappears after failing

Conflicting reports — Unknown

Reports conflict here. AlterEgoGenral believes a failed quest can simply be retaken, but aes1218 ("Large Egg Quest Gone", Aug 3) failed the escape twice and reports the quest disappearing entirely, with no found way to re-trigger it. Whether a failed Gigi quest can always be re-accepted is currently Unknown. Our site recommendation: make a manual save before handing over the eggs, so a failed escape costs you a reload instead of the quest line. See our failed-quest replay page for the general recovery route.

Is there anything after this quest?

Corroborated by players

Multiple players report that no further quest has appeared in the current build. After you deliver Gigi home she says she will contact you, and then nothing follows. Players confirm the silence is widespread rather than a personal bug: Kapone ("not implemented yet") and Flaminguo ("last one for now") in this thread, and in a second thread players report waiting with no follow-up — MeadRun to Summer day 10, antoineb.lacasse into Autumn, plus Rookar and Seter with the same silence. If you are wondering "Gigi never called — is my game bugged?", the player reports say no: no continuation has surfaced in the current build.

First-try success checklist (our consolidated route)

  1. Raise chicken ratings by petting daily until large eggs start dropping; keep a heater (from the chicken seller), food and water inside and outside the barn.
  2. Collect 2 large eggs — regular eggs do not count.
  3. Make a manual save before traveling to the hand-in.
  4. Take the subway to the hand-in. Leave your car at home.
  5. Escape the police on foot — run for the river to drop the drones, then under the ferris wheel toward the museum; let Gigi catch up after.
  6. Follow the mini-map star marker (it may not appear on the big map) all the way to Gigi's front door, and talk to her there to finish.

Gigi quest FAQ

Why do I only ever get regular eggs?

Large eggs are tied to your chickens' rating, not luck or coop count. Pet the chickens every day; once the rating rises, they start laying large eggs, and with 2 in hand the quest shows the delivery point.

Gigi keeps following me and won't get out / won't go home. What do I do?

Check the mini-map for a mission star marker — it can appear there while staying absent from the big map. Walk (do not drive) to that star, which is her house; she only becomes interactable at her front door. Reloads do not reliably fix this state for everyone, so the subway + on-foot route is the prevention.

Gigi said she'd contact me but never did. Is my quest line bugged?

Probably not. Multiple players report that no further quest has appeared after "Real Eggs, Real Evidence" in the current Early Access build — players waited into Summer and Autumn with no follow-up. We treat the line as ending here unless official notes or new reports show otherwise.
