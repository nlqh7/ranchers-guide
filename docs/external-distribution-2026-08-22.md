## Steam Discussion

### Title

What happens after the two-egg quest in The Ranchers?

### Body

I checked the discussion about the quest after handing in the two eggs. The current official answer is narrower than “there are no more quests”: the two-egg hand-in is the end of the main questline for now, and the developer team plans to expand that line and attach quests to future mechanics.

That means:

- finishing the egg route can be a legitimate current stopping point;
- it does not prove that every optional or unrelated quest has been catalogued;
- an update can move this boundary, so old “hidden trigger” guesses should not be treated as instructions.

Source: https://steamcommunity.com/app/1501310/discussions/0/590686908802417907/
