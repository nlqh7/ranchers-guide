## High-Value Leads

1. **Crop seed price and yield tests** — Steam Community Guides currently surfaces a guide with this scope. This is a strong opportunity because the site's crop dataset intentionally leaves per-crop sell prices blank when the source only exposes aggregate settlement totals. We can add a comparison only after two independent current-build records or a controlled test.
2. **Animal husbandry and production** — The Steam guide index surfaces an animal husbandry guide. Use it as a checklist for acquisition, housing, feed, reproduction and output; do not copy its prose or promote Alpha Wiki tables without current evidence.
3. **Opening hours** — Two community guides cover shop hours. Treat old Wiki/Steam guides as historical until a current-build screenshot or two current reports agree.
4. **Building visual checklist** — The official Wiki's Barn, Coop, Silo and Water Tower pages expose the right visual questions: exterior tier, interior, door state, grid coverage, storage/feeding connections and blueprint panel. These are better targets for original screenshots than generic decorative images.

## Image Policy

- Do not copy images from the official Wiki, Steam Community Guides, BWIKI, MapGenie or other fan sites into the public site merely because they are reachable.
- Do not use AI-generated artwork as a replacement for the real game map or as evidence of a location.
- Prefer screenshots captured from the current Steam build, with the page stating the build and what the screenshot proves.
- Use official Press Kit assets only after obtaining explicit permission for third-party commercial reuse; the Press Kit page itself does not grant that permission.
- If an external image is ever approved, record its source URL, owner, licence/permission, retrieval date and any required attribution beside the asset.
