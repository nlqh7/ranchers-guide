# Crop Calendar Evidence Gate - August 2026

## Decision

Do not publish a crop calendar yet. Keep the route absent from the sitemap and search index until the minimum current-build evidence below is retained.

## Why It Is on Hold

- Only Red Lettuce and Strawberry have readable launch-build timing cards.
- The seed-card phrase “3 days” or “7 days” does not prove whether planting day counts as day zero or day one.
- Strawberry's day 7, 10, 13 schedule is a labeled theoretical allocation model, not a retained playthrough timeline.
- Crop loss near the season boundary has two reports, but the exact day-30 to day-1 cutoff comes from one explanation.
- Most crops still lack current-build season, first-harvest, regrow, yield, and sale-channel observations.

## Publication Gate

For at least three crops, retain all of the following on the same named current build:

1. Seed listing with season, stated growth time, and regrow wording.
2. Planting date and approximate in-game time.
3. First harvest date and yield.
4. Second harvest date for a recurring crop, or confirmed plant removal for a one-time crop.
5. One season-boundary control that proves whether the crop survives or is removed.
6. Screenshots or a continuous short recording that can be reviewed later.

Only then may the public calendar compute planting deadlines. Until that gate passes, the crop database and farming guide remain the correct user-facing surfaces.
