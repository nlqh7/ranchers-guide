## Title

The Ranchers 0.8.10.842: what to recheck after the hotfix

## Post

If a quest or animal state looked wrong before the latest hotfix, update to **0.8.10.842 or later** before trying an older workaround again.

### What this update changes for players

- Some quest progression and validation failures are covered by the update.
- Several coop and barn edge cases are addressed, including association problems, growth-stage state, hunger display, linked counts, repeated turning at doors and one same-frame disappearance case.
- Shop and crafting batch limits are higher: stackable shop items can reach 200, non-stackable purchases 50, and crafting batches 50.

### A practical recheck order

1. Confirm the build number on the title screen.
2. Reopen the affected quest and record whether the objective validates before trying to replay the whole chain.
3. For an animal issue, check the coop or barn link and linked count first, then record the animal's growth and hunger state.
4. If the issue still happens, save the exact build, location, door state and reproduction steps. A residual report is much more useful than “the animal disappeared”.

This update does **not** confirm exact crop prices, fertilizer calculations, greenhouse unlocking, map coordinates or every building-placement rule. Those questions still need their own current-build evidence.
