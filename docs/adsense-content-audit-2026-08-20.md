## Decision

Do not build a new Wiki layer, add tools, or expand entity counts before addressing the content risks in this report. The site already has sound technical hygiene. AdSense approval still depends on whether indexed pages provide original, substantial, satisfying help rather than looking like templates, research notes, or incomplete datasets.

Google does not publish an approval score or a guaranteed page/traffic threshold. This audit is a prioritization tool, not a prediction of approval.

## Method

The audit covers all 54 sitemap URLs plus four intentional noindex surfaces. Gameplay pages are scored from 0-2 for: direct answer, current-build value, original value, actionable steps, traceable evidence, useful next step, and finished feel. Maximum: 14. `11-14` = strong; `8-10` = useful but improvable; `0-7` = review before resubmission. Trust and hub pages are judged by role rather than gameplay depth.

## Competitive Reality Check

- The official Ranchers Wiki already covers controls, ranching, farming, building, animals, barns/coops, vehicles, quests, multiplayer, weather, seasons, shops, points of interest and items. A conventional encyclopedia clone would duplicate an established source.
- Heartopia sites advertise hundreds of items and 300+ map markers. Their universal search, trackers and map work because the underlying dataset is dense. Copying those interfaces now would magnify this site's sparsity.
- `the-ranchers.wiki` contains broad generic advice and is not an accuracy benchmark without source-level verification.

The defensible position remains: official sources explain what exists; this site should help a player complete a current task or recover from a current problem.

## Contribution Delivery Finding

The public contribution page is not a hosted submission system. It validates fields in the browser, builds a structured message, and opens a `mailto:` draft addressed to `contribute@theranchersguide.com`. It has no server-side receipt, storage, delivery acknowledgement, or submission count.

On 2026-08-20 an explicit test sent the exact form-shaped payload from the connected Gmail account to `contribute@theranchersguide.com`. Gmail recorded the outbound message but no separate forwarded copy appeared. Cloudflare then sent a routing diagnostic notice confirming that Gmail commonly deduplicates messages when the sender and routing destination are the same account, and recommended testing from a different address. This resolves the missing-copy symptom; it does not constitute a distinct-sender end-to-end delivery test.

Before presenting the route as reliable, replace the `mailto:` dependency with a same-origin POST endpoint, validate abuse protection server-side, return a visible receipt ID, and retain a minimal review record. Keep email forwarding as notification rather than the sole data path.

No approval is guaranteed. The goal is to remove obvious low-value and unfinished-content signals, not to manufacture a passing score.
